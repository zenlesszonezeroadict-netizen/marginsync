------------------------------------------------------------
 DESCRIPTION  (the short blurb under the tagline)
------------------------------------------------------------
MarginSync is a Shopify app for stores that price from
supplier CSV or Excel files. Upload the file, it auto-detects
your SKU and cost columns, matches them to your catalogue,
and shows a full preview before any price changes. One click
to apply, one click to roll back. Free during beta.

 FIRST COMMENT  (post this yourself the moment you launch —

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I'm Hughez. I'm 14, based in Jakarta, and I built MarginSync
with no coding background using AI tooling.

The problem: stores that buy from real suppliers get price
lists as CSV or Excel files, and updating those prices in
Shopify means matching SKUs and re-typing numbers by hand for
hours. One typo quietly wrecks a margin for a month.

MarginSync fixes that. You upload the supplier file exactly
as it arrived. It figures out your SKU and cost columns,
matches them to your live catalogue, and shows you every
price change BEFORE anything goes live. Apply in one click,
roll back in one click, everything logged.

It's completely free while it's in beta. I'd genuinely love
feedback from anyone who runs a store:
 - Would you actually use this, or do you price a different way?
 - What would make you trust it with your live prices?
 - Anything that would stop you trying it?

I'll be here all day answering everything. Thanks for taking
a look 🙏
