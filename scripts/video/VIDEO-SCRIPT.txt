============================================================
 MarginSync — video script & recording structure
============================================================
Format: vertical (9:16). Length: 25-40 seconds. Record each
CLIP separately on your phone, in portrait.

Why this structure: short-form algorithms in 2026 reward
watch-time. They decide whether to push your video based on
the first 1-3 seconds, and reward videos people finish
(~70% completion is the goal). So we hook hard, show proof
fast, and end with a clear ask. Structure = HOOK > BUILD > PAYOFF.

------------------------------------------------------------
 CLIP 1 — THE HOOK   (0-3 seconds)   << most important
------------------------------------------------------------
Say it straight to camera, fast, no intro:

  "I'm 14 and I built a Shopify app with zero coding
   experience. Here's what it does."

Alternatives (test different ones across videos):
  - "A 14-year-old built this Shopify tool. Watch."
  - "Store owners are wasting 3 hours a week on this. I
     fixed it at 14 with no coding background."

RULES:
  - No "Hey guys, welcome back." You have 2 seconds. Use them.
  - Make a bold, specific claim. Numbers help ("14", "3 hours").
  - Show your face for the hook. Faces hold attention.

------------------------------------------------------------
 CLIP 2 — THE BUILD / PROOF   (3-30 seconds)
------------------------------------------------------------
Screen-record MarginSync actually working while you talk
over it. Show the OUTCOME, not a feature tour:

  "If your supplier sends you a price list, you used to
   update every product by hand. With MarginSync you upload
   the file, it matches your products, shows you exactly
   what changes BEFORE anything goes live, and you apply it
   in one click. If something's wrong, one click undoes it."

WHAT TO SHOW ON SCREEN (in this order):
  1. The supplier CSV file
  2. Uploading it into MarginSync
  3. The preview screen with the price changes
  4. Clicking sync / the success state

Keep it moving. Cut any dead air. 20-25 seconds max here.

------------------------------------------------------------
 CLIP 3 — THE PAYOFF / CTA   (last 3-6 seconds)
------------------------------------------------------------
Back to your face:

  "It's completely free while it's in beta. Comment 'price'
   and I'll send you the link."

Why "comment a word": comments tell the algorithm people are
engaging, which pushes the video to more people. Replying to
each comment with the link boosts it even more.

Alternative CTA:
  - "Link's in my bio — it's free right now."

------------------------------------------------------------
 IMPORTANT — what NOT to say
------------------------------------------------------------
Do NOT mention the cold-email outreach bot. Talking publicly
about sending automated cold emails makes the business look
like spam and can attract the wrong attention. The video is
about the PRODUCT and your story, nothing else.

------------------------------------------------------------
 After recording
------------------------------------------------------------
Name the clips 01_hook.mp4 / 02_demo.mp4 / 03_cta.mp4, drop
them in the  raw  folder, and run make-video.bat.
Post the result to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts the same day.
