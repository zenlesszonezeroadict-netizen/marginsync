 CAPTIONS ON THE VIDEO ITSELF (burned-in text)
------------------------------------------------------------
Captions on-screen massively boost watch time. Two options:
  - Run install-auto-captions.bat once, and make-video.bat
    will transcribe and burn them in for you automatically.
  - Or write them by hand in captions.srt (template below).

Whichever you use, the FIRST words on screen should be your
hook ("I'm 14 and I built a Shopify app...") so people who
watch with sound off still get pulled in.
