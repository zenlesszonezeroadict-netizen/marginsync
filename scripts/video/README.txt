============================================================
 MarginSync Auto-Editor — how to use it
============================================================

This turns your raw phone clips into ONE finished vertical
video (9:16) ready to post on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and
YouTube Shorts. You don't need any editing skills.

------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 1 — Record your clips on your phone
------------------------------------------------------------
Film in PORTRAIT (hold the phone upright). Record each part
as a SEPARATE short clip. Don't worry about mistakes — just
re-record a clip if you flub it.

Follow this structure (see VIDEO-SCRIPT.txt for the words):
   Clip 1  = HOOK      (0-3 sec)   grab attention instantly
   Clip 2  = BUILD     (3-30 sec)  show the product working
   Clip 3  = PAYOFF/CTA (last 5s)  tell them what to do next

------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 2 — Move the clips onto your computer
------------------------------------------------------------
Put them in the  raw  folder (next to this file).
NAME THEM IN ORDER so the editor knows the sequence:
   01_hook.mp4
   02_demo.mp4
   03_cta.mp4
(Any names work as long as they sort in order — 01, 02, 03.)

------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 3 (optional) — Add music
------------------------------------------------------------
Drop ONE .mp3 into the  music  folder. It gets mixed in
quietly under your voice. Skip this if you don't want music.
(Use royalty-free / TikTok-safe audio.)

------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 4 (optional) — Captions
------------------------------------------------------------
Captions massively boost watch time. Two ways:
  A) AUTOMATIC: run  install-auto-captions.bat  once. After
     that, the editor writes and burns captions for you.
  B) MANUAL: create a file called  captions.srt  yourself.

------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 5 — Build the video
------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click  make-video.bat .
Your finished video appears in:  output\finished.mp4
The output folder opens automatically when it's done.

------------------------------------------------------------
 Posting tips
------------------------------------------------------------
- Post the SAME finished.mp4 to TikTok, Reels, AND Shorts.
- Reply to every comment fast — it boosts the video.
- The "I'm 14, built a Shopify app, no coding" angle is your
  hook. Lead with it. Do NOT mention the cold-email bot.
